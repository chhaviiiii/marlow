# Marlow

Marlow is the shop agent for Field & Grain, a fictional furniture workshop in Austin. Its buyers are AI agents. A merchant can also set up a shop of their own at `/setup` with a catalog and a policy.

- Mechanism: policy sets the price, a signed mandate admits the buyer, PayPal holds the deposit, and a paid hold moves the workshop schedule.
- Audience: PayPal AI Hackathon judges watching a 1440x900 demo video, and anyone running the live demo on a laptop or phone.
- Register: product. The visitor runs a sale, so every step must stay easy to follow.
- Surfaces: Agent, Floor, Books, Catalog, Rules, Set up, and a shop page at `/m/<id>` for each created shop.
- Truth to keep: all demo options, PayPal's own button, sample data shown as sample data, green for pass, red for refusal.
- Brand personality: a working shop. Exact, plain, proud of its records.
- Anti-references: templated AI dashboards, chat bubbles, pill chips, centered welcome screens with card grids.
